ගණනය කරන ආකාරය · How the charts are calculated

  • Planet positions: Swiss Ephemeris, which is built on NASA JPL's ephemeris (compared above).
  • Ayanamsa: Lahiri (Chitrapaksha), the standard used by the Government of India's Rashtriya Panchang.
  • Houses: whole-sign (rashi) houses; Rahu and Ketu are the mean nodes.
  • Sri Lankan clock changes: Sri Lanka's standard time changed several times (for example UTC+6:00 and +6:30 between 1996 and 2006). The time zone database applies the right offset for each birth date, so enter the time as recorded.
